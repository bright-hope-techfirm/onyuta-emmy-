Welcome, and thank you for visiting Bright Hope Tech Firm. We are glad you are here.

This platform is dedicated to digital inclusion, accessibility software resources, and practical support for visually impaired learners and the wider community across East Africa.

Founded by Emmy Onyuta, a Bachelor of Information Technology student at St. Peter’s University, Uganda, and a person living with visual impairment.

Our Mission

Bright Hope Tech Firm exists to break digital barriers for persons with visual impairment. We provide reliable information, user manuals, installation guides, and updates for accessibility tools such as NVDA, TalkBack, and JAWS. We also deliver specialised computer training and outreach programmes to learning institutions across East Africa.

Our long-term vision is to promote self-reliance, independent living, and equal participation in the digital world for visually impaired people while serving the general public with professional ICT services.

Meet the Founder

Emmy Onyuta was born in 1999 in Oguru, Unyama, Gulu City. After losing his sight in early childhood due to eye cancer and navigating the challenges of displacement during the LRA insurgency, he learned Braille and pursued education through inclusive and specialised schools. He is currently studying Information Technology at St. Peter’s University with a clear purpose: to use technology to empower others facing similar barriers.
